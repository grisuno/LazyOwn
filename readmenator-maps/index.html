How to read these maps

Open any map, then: drag nodes freely while physics settles the rest, search (/) to filter, click a node to focus it with full file documentation, Upstream and Downstream to trace authored reach, Path to probe the exact route between two ids, Lens to compare semantic roles, Play to walk the guided chapters, Stabilize to re-run physics, Theme for dark and light, Export for PNG or typed JSON. Deep links such as #route=a~b restore any reading.
